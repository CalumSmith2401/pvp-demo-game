You and your rival are dealt the same eight rolls, one at a time. Put each roll in one of your eight zones. It stays there. When the table is full, every zone pays out and the higher total wins.

How a match works
  • Three dice are rolled. Both players see the same roll.
  • Tap an empty zone to place the roll there, or drag the dice onto it. You can't move it afterwards.
  • Each zone pays by its own rule, printed on the zone. The number in its corner is what this roll would score there.
  • Your rival's choice stays hidden until you have both placed. Then you see where their dice went.
  • You have seconds per roll. Run out and the roll goes into your weakest open zone.
  • After eight rolls the zones are scored one by one. Level totals go to whoever won more zones.
