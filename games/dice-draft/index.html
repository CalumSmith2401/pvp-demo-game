How to play

  1. Three dice are rolled. You and your rival see the same roll.
  2. Tap an empty zone to place the roll there, or drag the dice onto it. You can't move it afterwards.
  3. Each zone pays by its own rule, printed on the zone. The number in its corner is what this roll would score there.
  4. Your rival's choice stays hidden until you have both placed. Then you see where their dice went.
  5. You have seconds per roll. Run out and the roll goes into your weakest open zone.
  6. After eight rolls the zones are scored one by one. Higher total wins; level totals go to whoever won more zones.
