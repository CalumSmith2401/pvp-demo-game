Dice and chip sounds: Casino Audio 1.1 by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), licensed CC0
(http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Trimmed of leading silence,
mixed to mono and converted from Ogg Vorbis to AAC (.m4a) so every browser,
including older Safari on iPhone, can play them. The file names are Kenney's.

The chimes, clock tick and win/lose stings are synthesised in js/sound.js.
